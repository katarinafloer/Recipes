Steamed Chicken with Ginger Spring Onion Sauce

Notes

Place a small bowl upside down in the center of the steaming dish -- this creates a vacuum that captures the chicken essence as it cooks. Use it in the sauce for the richest flavor.

Instructions

  1. Place a small bowl upside down in the center of a larger heatproof bowl. Arrange chicken drumsticks around it. Scatter ginger slices and spring onion batons over the top.
  2. Steam chicken for 20-25 minutes.
  3. While chicken steams, combine finely chopped spring onion, diced garlic, grated ginger, and salt in a heatproof bowl.
  4. Heat oil in a small pot until lightly smoking, then pour over the aromatics.
  5. Once chicken is cooked, remove drumsticks and let rest. Lift the inverted bowl to reveal the captured chicken essence.
  6. Combine chicken essence with the aromatic mixture. Stir in soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  7. Thinly slice drumsticks, toss in the sauce with coriander, and serve with rice.
