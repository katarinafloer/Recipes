Pasta with Shallot, Peas & Gorgonzola Cream

Dinner · 5 minutes · 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 shallots, finely sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 100 g gorgonzola, crumbled
  • 150 ml cooking cream
  • juice of half a lemon
  • butter, for cooking
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • parmesan, to serve

Date Cooked

  • 2026-08-06

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté the shallots until soft and golden, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the peas and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Pour in the cream and add the gorgonzola. Stir until the cheese melts into a smooth sauce.
  6. Add the drained pasta and toss, loosening with pasta water as needed.
  7. Finish with lemon juice and black pepper. Serve with parmesan.
