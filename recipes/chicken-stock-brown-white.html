Chicken Stock (Brown & White)

Dinner · 10 minutes · 10

Source

Ingredients

  • chicken bones (about 2-4 lbs)
  • 1 large carrot, rough chop
  • 1 large onion, rough chop
  • 2 ribs celery, rough chop
  • 5 cloves garlic, rough chop (white stock)
  • 1 bay leaf (white stock)
  • oil
  • water

Date Cooked

  • 2026-10-03
  • 2026-10-04

Notes

Do not season with salt or pepper -- season when using in a recipe. Reducing a pre-salted stock will make it too salty.

Two methods: brown stock (roasted bones, deeper flavor) and white stock (blanched bones, cleaner/lighter).

Instructions

### Brown Stock

  1. Drizzle chicken bones with oil and roast on a baking rack at 425F for 40-45 minutes.
  2. Transfer bones to a large pot. Add carrot, onion, and celery.
  3. Cover with water by 1 inch. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a low simmer.
  4. Skim any scum from the surface. Simmer 2-4 hours.
  5. Remove bones, then strain through a fine mesh sieve.

### White Stock

  1. Place bones in a large pot, cover with cold water. Bring to a rapid boil for 10 seconds.
  2. Strain and rinse bones with water. Return to pot and cover with fresh water.
  3. Add onion, celery, garlic, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a light simmer.
  4. Skim scum as it forms. Simmer 3-4 hours.
  5. Strain and discard bones and vegetables.
