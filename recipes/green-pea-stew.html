Green Pea Stew

Lunch/Dinner · 20 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

  • 450g frozen or fresh green peas
  • 400g tomato puree (about 14 oz)
  • 2 tbsp sunflower oil
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 4 stalks celery, thinly sliced
  • 2 large carrots, cut into medallions
  • 6 small potatoes, cut into small cubes
  • 1 cup fresh dill, roughly chopped
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 2 tsp cimbru (or thyme)
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt, or more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper (or black pepper)

Date Cooked

  • 2026-11-17

Instructions

  1. Heat oil over medium heat. Add onion and cook until browned.
  2. Add carrots and celery and saute another 3 minutes.
  3. Add potatoes and 400ml (1.5 cups) water. Cover and cook on high heat for 8 minutes or until potatoes are fork tender.
  4. Add tomato puree and all seasoning except dill. Stir to combine, cover, and cook on stovetop or in a 200C/400F oven for 30-35 minutes until vegetables are soft and sauce has thickened.
  5. Remove from heat and stir in chopped dill, reserving some for garnish.
  6. Taste and adjust salt. Serve hot with crusty bread.
