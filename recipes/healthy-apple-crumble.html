Healthy Apple Crumble

Desserts · 10 minutes · 2

Source

Ingredients

  • 2 apples, chopped
  • cinnamon, to taste
  • 1-1.5 tbsp honey
  • 60g oats
  • 50g light butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • yogurt, ice cream, or custard, to serve

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180C/350F.
  2. Add chopped apples to a baking dish with cinnamon and honey. Mix until coated.
  3. Bake for 10 minutes to let the apples start to soften.
  4. Meanwhile, mix oats, butter, and brown sugar in a bowl until crumbly and clumpy.
  5. Remove apples from oven and scatter crumble mixture evenly over the top. Gently press down to cover the apples.
  6. Bake for another 20 minutes until topping is golden brown.
  7. Serve warm with yogurt, ice cream, or custard.
