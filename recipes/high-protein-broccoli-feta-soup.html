High-Protein Broccoli Feta Soup

Dinner · 15 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

  • 5 cups (450g) broccoli florets
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 small yellow onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 3 cups bone broth
  • 1/2 cup cottage cheese
  • 6 oz feta cheese
  • zest and juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • salt and pepper
  • fresh dill, for garnish (optional)
  • parmesan, for garnish (optional)

Date Cooked

  • 2026-08-18

Notes

Cottage cheese and bone broth are the protein hacks here - blends in completely and you can't taste it.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 415°F.
  2. Toss broccoli, onion, and garlic with olive oil, oregano, chili flakes, salt, and pepper. Tuck the feta in the corner of the baking tray. Roast for 28-30 minutes until golden and slightly crisp at the edges.
  3. Add roasted vegetables to a blender with bone broth, spinach, cottage cheese, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  4. Warm through in a pot. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  5. Garnish with fresh dill, chili flakes, and finely shredded parmesan.
