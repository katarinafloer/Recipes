## Notes

Not my favorite — wouldn't make again as-is.

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté the onion for 3-4 minutes until softened.
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the tuna and stir to combine.
4. Pour in the marinara sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, cook the gnocchi in salted boiling water until they float, about 2-3 minutes. Drain.
6. Toss the gnocchi in the sauce. Season with salt and black pepper and serve.
