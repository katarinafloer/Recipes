## Notes

Similar to Salisbury steak. The crushed ice keeps the patties tender and juicy.

Serve with creamy mashed potatoes and the caramelized onion gravy spooned over the top.

## Instructions

1. Dice the small onion and sauté in 1 tablespoon of butter until soft and translucent. Let cool.
2. In a bowl, combine the breadcrumbs with the water and milk. Let sit for a minute.
3. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef and pork. Fold in the crushed ice, breadcrumb mixture, egg, sautéed onion, salt, and pepper. Mix until combined. Form into patties.
4. Add the diced potatoes to a large pot of water. Bring to a boil and cook until fork tender.
5. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a large pan and brown the patties about 5 minutes per side until cooked through, working in batches. Remove and set aside.
6. In the same pan, add the sliced onions and a teaspoon of salt. Stir often for about 10 minutes, then add the beef broth and soy sauce. Continue cooking until the onions are caramelized.
7. Add the patties back into the pan for a couple of minutes until warmed through.
8. Mash the cooked potatoes with butter and milk. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
