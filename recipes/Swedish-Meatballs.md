## Notes

Linger/cranberry note: lingonberries are traditional and most IKEAs sell them. Cranberries are a substitute but not the same. Chill meatballs for 1 hour before cooking to help them hold shape.

## Instructions

### Meatballs

1. Combine panko, heavy cream, milk, egg, and Dijon in a bowl. Mix and let sit 5 minutes.
2. Add ground beef, ground pork, allspice, salt, and pepper. Mix until well combined. Form into golf-ball-sized meatballs and chill 1 hour.
3. Sear meatballs in a pan with olive oil. Butter baste with thyme and rosemary until fully cooked. Remove from pan.

### Gravy

4. In the same pan, reduce beef stock with heavy cream until thickened. Whisk in Dijon mustard.
5. Return meatballs to pan and warm in the gravy before serving.

### Mashed Potatoes

6. Boil potatoes from cold water for about 35 minutes until tender. Peel while warm and pass through a sieve.
7. Add to a pan over low heat and gradually fold in butter until creamy. Pour in milk, season with salt, and fold gently.

### Cranberry Sauce

8. Cook lingonberries/cranberries in a pan over medium heat with sugar and lemon juice until jam-like. Do not squish the berries.

### Quick Pickles

9. In a saucepan over low heat, dissolve sugar with white wine vinegar, water, and mustard seeds.
10. Add sliced cucumbers and dill. Transfer to a container.
