Blueberry & Lemon Olive Oil Cake

Desserts · 15 minutes · 8

Source

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zested
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Serve with Greek yogurt and extra lemon zest.

Toss blueberries in a little flour before folding in - keeps them from sinking to the bottom.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375F.
  2. Mix wet ingredients together: eggs, sugar, olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, vanilla, and milk. Add chia seeds and let sit for a few minutes.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients and mix well.
  5. Fold in blueberries.
  6. Pour into a greased loaf or round cake pan and bake for about 1 hour, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Let cool before slicing. Serve with Greek yogurt and lemon zest.
