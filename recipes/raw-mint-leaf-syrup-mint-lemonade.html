Raw Mint Leaf Syrup & Mint Lemonade

Drinks · 15 minutes · 1 jar

Source

Ingredients

  • fresh mint leaves
  • white granulated sugar (equal parts to mint)
  • 2-3 lemons (for syrup)
  • sparkling water (for lemonade)
  • ice

Date Cooked

  • 2026-09-12

Notes

Use white granulated sugar only - other sugars alter the taste.

If it takes longer to dissolve, add a little water to help.

Ready when sugar dissolves and syrup turns light green (48-72 hours).

Instructions

  1. Wash mint and strip leaves from stems. Roughly chop to release aroma.
  2. In a jar, layer equal parts chopped mint and sugar. Add a generous drizzle of lemon juice (2-3 lemons). Press gently.
  3. Close lid and refrigerate for 48-72 hours, stirring once a day.
  4. Strain and store in the fridge.

For mint lemonade: fill a glass with ice, add juice of one lemon, a pour of mint syrup, and top with sparkling water. Mix and enjoy.
