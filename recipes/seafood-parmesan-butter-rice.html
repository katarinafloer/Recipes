Seafood Parmesan Butter Rice

Dinner · 5 minutes · 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked rice
  • 250-350 g mixed seafood
  • 2-3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/3-1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan
  • fresh parsley, chopped
  • fresh dill, chopped
  • juice of 1/2 lemon
  • black pepper
  • salt

Date Cooked

  • 2026-07-24

Notes

Reserve a little starchy rice water before draining — it helps make the rice glossy and creamy when you stir in the Parmesan.

Fold the seafood back in at the very end so it doesn't overcook. The lemon is important — it balances the richness of the butter and Parmesan.

Unconventional to use Parmesan with seafood in Italian cooking, but it works well here.

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to package directions, reserving a splash of the starchy cooking water before draining.
  2. Sauté the seafood in butter and garlic over medium-high heat until just cooked. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  3. Add the cooked rice to the same pan with a splash of reserved rice water and a little more butter.
  4. Turn the heat to low, then stir in the grated Parmesan until the rice is glossy and slightly creamy.
  5. Fold the seafood back in gently.
  6. Finish with lots of parsley, a smaller amount of dill, black pepper, and lemon juice. Season with salt to taste.
