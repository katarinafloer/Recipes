## Notes

Hokkaido pumpkin works best. If the pumpkin is very watery, squeeze out the excess moisture but don't dry it completely. 100g is the sweet spot -- enough for color and moisture without making the rolls soggy.

## Instructions

### Evening

1. Finely grate the pumpkin. Squeeze out excess liquid if very watery.
2. Combine both flours, skyr, grated pumpkin, and salt in a bowl.
3. Dissolve yeast and honey in 300ml cold water, then add to the flour mixture.
4. Mix into a soft, slightly sticky dough. If the dough seems too firm, add the remaining 20ml water.
5. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for 12-18 hours.

### Morning

6. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured or semolina-dusted surface.
7. With damp hands or a dough scraper, portion into 9-10 pieces. Do not knead -- this preserves the gas bubbles in the dough.
8. Sprinkle with pumpkin seeds if desired.

### Baking

9. Preheat oven to 220C. For steam, pour a splash of hot water into a preheated heatproof dish at the bottom of the oven.
10. Bake for 10 minutes, then reduce to 210C and bake a further 15-20 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
