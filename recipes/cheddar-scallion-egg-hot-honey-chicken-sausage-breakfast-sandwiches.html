Cheddar Scallion Egg & Hot Honey Chicken Sausage Breakfast Sandwiches

Breakfast · 20 minutes · 10

Source

Ingredients

  • 15 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup scallions, chopped
  • 10 English muffins
  • butter
  • 2 lbs ground chicken
  • 1/3 cup hot honey
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 10 slices sharp cheddar cheese
  • olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Great for meal prep - wrap in parchment and freeze. Reheat from fridge at 400F for 10 min, from frozen about 25 min.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F. Whisk together eggs, milk, scallions, 1/2 tsp salt and black pepper.
  2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with greased parchment. Pour in the eggs and bake for about 15 minutes until fully set. Cut into circles with the back of a glass, or slice into rectangles.
  3. Halve the English muffins, spread with butter, and bake for 6-8 minutes until lightly toasted.
  4. Mix ground chicken with hot honey, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, 1 tsp salt, and a pinch of black pepper. Do not overmix.
  5. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Form chicken into patties the width of your muffin, about 1/2 inch thick. Cook 5 minutes per side.
  6. Assemble: cheese on one muffin half, then sausage, then egg, then top half.
  7. To freeze: wrap in parchment and store in freezer bags.
