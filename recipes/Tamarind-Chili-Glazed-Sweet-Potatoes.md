## Notes

Can be made in an air fryer (18 min at 200C/400F) or oven (30 min at 200C/400F). Add pomegranate only when ready to serve.

## Instructions

1. Peel and cut sweet potatoes into 1/2 inch cubes.
2. In a large bowl, mix sweet potatoes with tamarind date chutney, olive oil, chili flakes, crushed coriander seeds, thyme, and salt until well coated.

**Air fryer:** Preheat at 200C/400F for 2 minutes. Air fry for 18 minutes, shaking halfway through.

**Oven:** Preheat at 200C/400F. Spread on a baking sheet and roast 30 minutes, flipping every 10 minutes.

3. Toss with fresh pomegranate before serving.
