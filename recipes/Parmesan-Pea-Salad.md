## Instructions

1. Season chicken breast with lemon pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Cook to taste.
2. Mix Greek yogurt, dijon mustard, garlic powder, onion powder, and lemon juice in a bowl.
3. Place frozen peas in a bowl, cover with water, and microwave for 3 minutes. Drain.
4. Add arugula to a serving bowl. Add drained peas.
5. Pour sauce over and mix through.
6. Dice chicken and add on top. Sprinkle with parmesan.
