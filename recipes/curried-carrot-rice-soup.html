Curried Carrot Rice Soup

Dinner · 15 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

Date Cooked

  • 2026-08-09

Notes

Blend the carrots, coconut milk, and vegetable broth until completely smooth for a creamy soup base.

The soup will thicken as the rice absorbs the liquid. Add additional vegetable broth or water if you prefer a thinner consistency.

Used lentils instead of chickpeas and added lime - worked well.

Instructions

  1. Cut the carrots into approximately 2 cm chunks.
  2. Place the carrots in a pot and add enough water to cover them.
  3. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to medium, and cook for about 15 minutes, or until the carrots are tender.
  4. Drain the carrots and transfer them to a blender.
  5. Add the coconut milk, vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Blend until completely smooth and creamy. Set aside.
  7. Cook the jasmine rice according to package directions and set aside.
  8. Heat a small amount of olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. Add the diced onion and sauté for 2-3 minutes, or until softened.
  10. Add the garlic, Thai red curry paste, and drained chickpeas.
  11. Cook for another 1-2 minutes, stirring frequently.
  12. Pour in the blended carrot soup mixture and add the cooked rice.
  13. Reduce the heat to low-medium and cook for 5-10 minutes, stirring regularly, until the soup has thickened slightly and is heated through.
  14. Remove from the heat and serve hot.
  15. Garnish with coconut cream, fresh herbs, and crushed nuts if desired.
