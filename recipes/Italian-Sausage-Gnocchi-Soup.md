## Notes

Gnocchi absorbs the broth as it sits - add a splash of broth or cream when reheating leftovers.

Can sub kale for spinach, or use spicy Italian sausage for extra heat.

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook diced onion until translucent.
2. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
3. Add sausage and break apart as it browns until cooked through.
4. Add tomato paste and all seasonings. Stir until evenly coated.
5. Pour in bone broth and bring to a boil.
6. Add dry gnocchi and cook for 2-3 minutes until just cooked through.
7. Reduce heat to low. Stir in heavy cream, then add parmesan and stir until melted and smooth.
8. Fold in chopped spinach.
9. Simmer on low for 10 minutes to thicken. Serve with extra parmesan and crusty bread.
