## Notes

Serve with crusty sourdough or toasted bread for scooping.

Tomato paste is the key to getting a thick, rich sauce.

## Instructions

1. Place halved tomatoes cut-side down in a pan with olive oil. Season with salt, cover, and cook on medium-low for 5-8 minutes until skins loosen.
2. Peel the skins off with tongs, then smash the tomatoes directly in the pan.
3. Add garlic and cook for 30-60 seconds until fragrant.
4. Stir in tomato paste and garlic confit. Mash and mix into a chunky sauce.
5. Add a generous amount of parmesan and stir until melted and creamy.
6. Make three wells and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook on medium-low for about 5 minutes until whites are set and yolks are still jammy.
7. Remove lid, add another layer of parmesan, crack black pepper, and scatter parsley. Cook uncovered 1 more minute.
8. Serve hot straight from the pan.
