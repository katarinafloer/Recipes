Baked Butternut with Spiced Pears

Notes

Butternut squash can vary in sweetness -- add a drizzle of maple syrup if you want more sweetness. Can be made ahead and refrigerated overnight, then reheated in the morning. For single serving: 185g puree, 1 egg, 1 tbsp oat flour, 1/2 tsp vanilla, 1/2 tsp cinnamon -- bake 35 minutes only.

To make butternut puree: halve squash, scoop seeds, roast cut side down at 200C/400F for 40 minutes. Scoop out flesh and blend until smooth. Can also use canned.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 175C/350F.
  2. Whisk together butternut puree, eggs, oat flour, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
  3. Pour into a baking dish and bake 40-45 minutes until set. Rest 5 minutes.
  4. While it bakes, combine pears, cardamom, ginger, salt, and water in a small pot. Cover and stew on low for 15 minutes until soft.
  5. Mix skyr with protein powder for the topping.
  6. Top baked butternut with yogurt-protein topping, stewed pears, and chopped pecans.
