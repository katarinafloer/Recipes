Jalapeno Honey Lime Steak Salad

Lunch · 15 minutes · 2

Source

Ingredients

  • steak (any cut)
  • corn (fresh, canned, or grilled)
  • avocado
  • cilantro
  • green onion
  • jalapeno, sliced
  • juice of 1-2 limes
  • honey
  • olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

No lettuce - more of a bowl-style salad. Works great with leftover steak.

Add cotija or feta on top if you want extra richness.

Instructions

  1. Season steak with salt and pepper. Cook to your preferred doneness, rest for 5 minutes, then slice thin.
  2. In a large bowl, combine corn, diced avocado, sliced jalapeno, chopped cilantro, and sliced green onion.
  3. Add the sliced steak on top.
  4. Drizzle generously with olive oil, lime juice, and honey.
  5. Toss gently and serve immediately.
