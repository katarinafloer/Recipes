Orange Posset

Desserts · 10 minutes · 6

Notes

Chill 2-4 hours before serving. No dairy-free substitutes -- the cream is essential for setting.

Optional: sprinkle sugar on top and caramelize with a torch before serving.

Instructions

  1. Halve oranges and scoop out the pulp. Set aside the orange shell halves to use as serving bowls.
  2. Heat heavy cream and sugar in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until sugar dissolves. Bring to a gentle boil and simmer for about 5 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in orange juice and zest.
  4. Pour into the hollowed orange halves or serving glasses.
  5. Refrigerate 2-4 hours until set.
