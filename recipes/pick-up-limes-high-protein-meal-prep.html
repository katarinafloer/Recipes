Pick Up Limes High-Protein Meal Prep

Lunch · 1 hour · 10

Source

Ingredients

  • 3 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1 1/3 cups long-grain white rice
  • 1/2 cup millet
  • 3 1/2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 tablespoon whole chia seeds
  • 600g firm tofu
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1/2 cup unsalted roasted almonds
  • 1/2 cup pickled red onions
  • 1/2 cup + 2 tablespoons teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tablespoons + 2 teaspoons sriracha
  • 6 1/2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1/4 lime, juiced
  • 1 2/3 cups canned light coconut milk
  • 3/4 cup coconut yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 large soft flour tortillas
  • 2 1/2 cups baby spinach
  • 1/4 cucumber
  • vegetable oil
  • salt

Notes

Makes 10 meals (5 recipes × 2 servings). Mix and match for breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Each dish takes about 5 minutes to assemble. Best consumed within 3-4 days.

Prep

  1. **Roasted sweet potatoes:** Preheat oven to 390°F. Poke sweet potatoes with a fork and bake 50-60 minutes. Leave 2 whole; peel and cube the third, add to cooked millet.
  2. **Rice:** Cook 1⅓ cups rice per package instructions (~15 minutes). Yields about 5 cups cooked.
  3. **Millet:** Combine millet and vegetable broth in a saucepan. Boil, reduce to simmer, cover and cook ~25 minutes stirring often until tender.
  4. **Berry chia jam:** Microwave berries at 600W for 3 minutes, stirring every minute. Stir in chia seeds. Let sit 30 minutes to gel.
  5. **Tofu:** Toss tofu cubes with cornstarch and salt. Cook in oil over medium-high heat 8-10 minutes until golden. Divide into 3 containers: plain, mild teriyaki (1 tbsp teriyaki + 1 tsp sriracha), saucy teriyaki (3 tbsp teriyaki + ½ tbsp sriracha).
  6. **Veggies:** Grate carrots and store in water. Store shredded cabbage separately. Roughly chop almonds.
  7. **Peanut sauce:** Whisk together 2½ tbsp peanut butter, 2 tbsp hot water, ½ tbsp teriyaki sauce, juice of ¼ lime, and 1 tsp sriracha.

Meal 1 - Sweet Potato Millet Congee

Serve millet with cubed sweet potato in bowls, top with mild teriyaki tofu, grated carrot, and pickled red onions. Heat through and add more sriracha if desired.

Meal 2 - Loaded Sweet Breakfast Potato

Slice roasted sweet potatoes lengthwise, microwave 2-3 minutes. Top with coconut yogurt, berry chia jam, peanut butter, chopped almonds, and cinnamon.

Meal 3 - Teriyaki Fried Rice

Sauté spinach, carrot, and cabbage in oil for 2 minutes. Add rice, plain tofu, teriyaki sauce, and sriracha. Cook 3 minutes. Top with pickled red onions and sesame seeds.

Meal 4 - Coconut Rice Porridge

Combine rice, coconut milk, maple syrup, and cinnamon in a saucepan. Boil, then simmer 5 minutes until creamy. Top with berry chia jam, coconut yogurt, peanut butter, and almonds.

Meal 5 - Teriyaki Tofu Veggie Wraps

Spread peanut sauce over tortillas. Fill with carrot, cabbage, spinach, cucumber, and saucy teriyaki tofu. Roll up and grill 1-2 minutes per side.
