## Notes

Protein powder is important for thick texture. Use oat flour for creamier results -- blend regular oats if needed.

## Instructions

1. Mix oat flour, protein powder, Greek yoghurt, flax seeds, peanut butter powder, and water together. Pour into a ramekin or bowl.
2. Refrigerate 1-2 hours to thicken.
3. Mix peanut butter and maple syrup together to make the caramel layer. Spread over the oats.
4. Melt dark chocolate in the microwave and drizzle over the top to form a shell. Add peanuts if desired.
5. Refrigerate overnight and enjoy.
