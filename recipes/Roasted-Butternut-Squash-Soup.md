## Notes

Use canned full fat coconut milk -- carton coconut milk will not make it creamy. Freezes well up to 3 months without toppings. For faster cooking, peel and cube the squash; it roasts in 30-35 minutes instead of 50-60.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 220C/425F. Score the cut sides of the squash in a deep crosshatch and place cut side up in a large roasting dish. Set an onion half cut side down into each seed cavity.
2. Add bell pepper, carrots, garlic heads (cut side up), and cherry tomatoes around the squash.
3. Drizzle everything with olive oil and sprinkle over cumin, smoked paprika, thyme, rosemary, salt, pepper, and chili flakes. Rub seasoning into the squash flesh.
4. Roast 50-60 minutes until a knife slides into the squash with no resistance, garlic is golden, and tomatoes have burst.
5. Let cool 5 minutes. Scoop squash flesh into a blender. Squeeze garlic out of their skins and add along with onion, pepper, carrots, tomatoes, and all the pan juices.
6. Add 1.5 cups broth and ginger. Blend until completely smooth, working in batches if needed.
7. Pour into a large pot over medium heat. Stir in coconut milk and warm 3-4 minutes. Add more broth to reach desired consistency. Taste and adjust salt.
8. Serve with a swirl of chili oil, drizzle of coconut milk, fresh herbs, and toasted sourdough.
