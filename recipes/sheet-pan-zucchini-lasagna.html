Sheet Pan Zucchini Lasagna

Dinner · 20 minutes · 8

Source

Ingredients

  • 3 large zucchini, sliced into 1/4" strips
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 1.5-2 cups whole milk ricotta
  • 10 oz mozzarella, grated
  • 1 lb ground beef, browned
  • 25 oz marinara sauce
  • 1 cup parmesan, freshly grated
  • 1/4 cup basil, chopped

Notes

Use thick whole milk ricotta. Let zucchini rest 15 minutes after pre-baking and drain any water from the pan before assembling -- this prevents a watery lasagna.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400F.
  2. Spread zucchini slices on a large rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt. Bake 10-15 minutes until tender. Rest 15 minutes, then move zucchini to a plate and drain any water from the pan.
  3. Spread 1 cup marinara over the pan. Layer half the zucchini on top in a single layer.
  4. Spread ricotta over zucchini, then layer with browned beef, half the remaining marinara, and half the mozzarella.
  5. Layer remaining zucchini, marinara, and mozzarella on top.
  6. Bake 15-20 minutes, broiling the last 2-3 minutes to crisp the cheese.
  7. Finish with freshly grated parmesan and chopped basil. Serve warm.
