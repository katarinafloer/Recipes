## Notes

Also works great served over noodles instead of rice.

If you don't want to make your own sauce, use 1 jar Trader Joe's Thai Peanut Satay Sauce — add water in the last step if needed to make it more saucy.

## Instructions

1. Heat toasted sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for about 1 minute.
2. Add the ground chicken and turn the heat up to medium-high. Use a wooden spoon or meat masher to break up the meat. Cook for 6-7 minutes, stirring often, until fully cooked and starting to brown.
3. While the chicken cooks, make the peanut sauce: whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sriracha, lime juice, honey, and water in a medium bowl.
4. Add the sauce to the skillet and cook for 1-2 minutes until the sauce slightly thickens. If it gets too thick, stir in more water. Taste and season with more soy sauce if desired.
5. Cook the rice according to package directions. Add rice to shallow bowls, then top with the chicken, cucumber, scallion, cilantro, chopped peanuts, and lime wedges.
