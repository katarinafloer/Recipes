Chicken Liver Pate

Snack/Appetizers · 5 minutes · 4

Ingredients

  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • chicken livers
  • butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • fresh parsley
  • hot water
  • salt and pepper

Date Cooked

  • 2026-10-03
  • 2026-10-04

Instructions

  1. Cook onion in butter in a pan over medium heat until softened.
  2. Add a splash of hot water and the chicken livers.
  3. Cook on medium-low heat for about 4 minutes per side.
  4. In the last 2 minutes, add the garlic.
  5. Transfer everything to a blender with butter and fresh parsley. Blend until smooth.
  6. Season with salt and pepper. Serve as a spread on bread.
