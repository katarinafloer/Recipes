Pickled Red Onions

Snack · 5 minutes · 8

Source

Ingredients

  • 2 medium red onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons agave syrup
  • 1 teaspoon coarse sea salt

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Best left overnight, but ready in 1-2 hours. Keeps in the fridge for 2-3 weeks.

Great on sandwiches, salads, nourish bowls, tacos, and curries.

Agave can be swapped for sugar or maple syrup.

Instructions

  1. Add the thinly sliced onions to a large heat-proof jar.
  2. Pour in the boiling water, vinegars, agave, and salt. Seal the jar and give it a few gentle shakes.
  3. Let cool to room temperature, then transfer to the fridge.
  4. Leave to pickle overnight for best results, or at least 1-2 hours.
