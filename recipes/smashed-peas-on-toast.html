Smashed Peas on Toast

Snack/Appetizers · 5 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

  • 250g frozen garden peas, thawed
  • 1.5 cloves garlic
  • 1 handful fresh mint leaves, chopped
  • 2 tsp tahini
  • 1/2 large lemon, juiced
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp chili powder (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • black pepper
  • toasted sourdough, for serving
  • chili flakes, for serving

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Pulse briefly in a food processor for texture, longer for smoother. Stores in fridge up to 4 days.

Also works as a dip, in falafel wraps, or alongside scrambled eggs.

Instructions

  1. Thaw peas by pouring boiled water over them, let sit a couple minutes, then drain and cool.
  2. Add peas, garlic, mint, tahini, lemon juice, olive oil, chili powder, salt, and pepper to a food processor. Pulse a few seconds, scrape down the sides, pulse again. Keep some texture or blend longer for smoother.
  3. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  4. Serve on toasted sourdough with a drizzle of olive oil and chili flakes.
