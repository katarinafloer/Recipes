Overnight Pumpkin Rolls

Breakfast · 20 minutes · 9-10

Source

Ingredients

  • 300g spelt flour type 630
  • 200g spelt flour type 1050
  • 150g plain skyr
  • 100g finely grated pumpkin (Hokkaido works best)
  • 300-320ml cold water
  • 4g fresh yeast
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 2 tsp salt (about 7g)
  • pumpkin seeds, for topping (optional)

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Hokkaido pumpkin works best. If the pumpkin is very watery, squeeze out the excess moisture but don't dry it completely. 100g is the sweet spot -- enough for color and moisture without making the rolls soggy.

Instructions

### Evening

  1. Finely grate the pumpkin. Squeeze out excess liquid if very watery.
  2. Combine both flours, skyr, grated pumpkin, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Dissolve yeast and honey in 300ml cold water, then add to the flour mixture.
  4. Mix into a soft, slightly sticky dough. If the dough seems too firm, add the remaining 20ml water.
  5. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for 12-18 hours.

### Morning

  1. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured or semolina-dusted surface.
  2. With damp hands or a dough scraper, portion into 9-10 pieces. Do not knead -- this preserves the gas bubbles in the dough.
  3. Sprinkle with pumpkin seeds if desired.

### Baking

  1. Preheat oven to 220C. For steam, pour a splash of hot water into a preheated heatproof dish at the bottom of the oven.
  2. Bake for 10 minutes, then reduce to 210C and bake a further 15-20 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
