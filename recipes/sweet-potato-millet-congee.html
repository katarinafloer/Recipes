Sweet Potato Millet Congee

Breakfast · 10 minutes · 2

Source

Ingredients

Date Cooked

  • 2026-11-21

Notes

To speed up: skip roasting and instead peel and cube the sweet potato, then add it directly to the pot with the millet.

Millet can vary in absorption -- cook down longer without lid if you want a thicker porridge.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200C/400F. Poke holes in the sweet potato with a fork and bake for about 50 minutes until easily pierced.
  2. Meanwhile, combine millet and broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook about 25 minutes stirring periodically until very tender.
  3. Toss tofu with cornstarch and salt. Heat oil in a pan over medium-high and cook tofu until golden on all sides. Stir in teriyaki sauce and sriracha.
  4. Peel the baked sweet potato, chop into cubes, and stir into the millet. Taste and add salt as needed.
  5. Serve topped with teriyaki tofu, grated carrot, pickled red onions, more sriracha, and green onions.
