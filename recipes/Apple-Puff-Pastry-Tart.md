## Notes

Prick the center well so the base stays flat under the filling.

Brush with apricot jam while still warm for a glossy finish.

Can be assembled up to 2 hours ahead and stored in the fridge before baking.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200C/400F. Line a baking sheet with parchment and unroll the pastry onto it.
2. Score a 1-2cm border around the edges without cutting through. Prick the center all over with a fork.
3. Peel apples and slice flesh away from cores in four sections. Lay flat and slice thinly. Sprinkle with a little brown sugar.
4. Mash together butter, brown sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt until smooth.
5. Spread cinnamon butter over the center of the pastry, inside the border. Arrange apple slices on top in rows or mounds. Brush the border with egg wash.
6. Bake 20-25 minutes until pastry is golden and crisp and apples are soft and caramelized.
7. Warm apricot jam for 15 seconds in the microwave, then brush over the warm tart. Serve warm or at room temperature.
