Apple Rings

Desserts · 10 minutes · 2

Source

Ingredients

  • 2 large firm apples
  • 1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed but still cold
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Slice apples crosswise into 1/2-inch thick rings. Cut out the core to create rings.
  3. Cut puff pastry into thin strips about 1/2-inch wide.
  4. Mix sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl.
  5. Wrap puff pastry strips around each apple ring, threading through the center until mostly covered.
  6. Roll each wrapped ring in the cinnamon sugar mixture, coating generously.
  7. Place on prepared baking sheet and bake 18-22 minutes until puffed and golden.
  8. Cool a few minutes before serving. Best enjoyed warm.
