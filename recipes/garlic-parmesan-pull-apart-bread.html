Garlic Parmesan Pull-Apart Bread

Snack/Appetizers · 20 minutes · 8

Source

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour
  • 1 tbsp dry yeast
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 250ml water
  • 50g canola oil
  • 1/2 tbsp salt
  • 1 cup peeled garlic cloves
  • olive oil, enough to cover garlic
  • 3 tbsp butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • parmesan, grated, for coating

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Knead thoroughly for a soft, fluffy texture. Roast the garlic slowly for a mellow, rich flavor. Don't worry about perfect shapes -- rustic pieces make the best pull-apart texture.

Instructions

  1. Combine yeast, sugar, and water in a bowl. Let sit a few minutes until foamy.
  2. Add flour, honey, and canola oil. Knead 3 minutes, then add salt. Continue kneading at least 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover and let rise at least 1 hour until doubled.
  4. Meanwhile, place garlic cloves in an oven-safe dish and cover completely with olive oil. Bake at 120C/250F until soft and golden (garlic confit).
  5. Transfer garlic to a bowl, mash, and mix with butter, parsley, chili flakes, and sesame seeds.
  6. Using kitchen scissors, cut risen dough into small rustic pieces.
  7. Toss each piece in the garlic butter, then coat with grated parmesan.
  8. Stack pieces in a baking pan and bake at 175C/350F for about 40 minutes until golden.
  9. Serve warm and pull apart.
