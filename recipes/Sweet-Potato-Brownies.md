## Notes

Should look slightly underdone when you take them out of the oven - that is correct.

Roasted sweet potato comes out sweeter than boiled.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350F.
2. Roast whole sweet potato at 400F for 50 minutes, then scoop and mash 1 cup.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together sweet potato, eggs, maple syrup, honey, and vanilla.
4. Add cacao powder, oat flour, salt, and baking soda. Stir with a spatula until combined.
5. Fold in chocolate chips.
6. Pour into a parchment-lined 8x8 baking dish. Sprinkle more chocolate chips on top.
7. Bake for 30-35 minutes. They should look slightly underdone - trust it.
8. Let cool before slicing.
