## Instructions

1. Add celery, apple, cucumber, parsley, microgreens, dates, and pecans to a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, mix together parmesan, dill, lemon juice, mayonnaise, avocado oil, salt, pepper, garlic, and maple syrup until smooth.
3. Pour dressing over salad and toss to combine.
