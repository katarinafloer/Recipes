One Pan Beef Dumplings

Notes

Salt the cabbage and squeeze out excess water before mixing -- this prevents a watery filling. Mix the filling until sticky and cohesive for a juicy, springy texture. Best served straight from the pan.

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the cabbage, sprinkle with salt, and leave a few minutes. Squeeze or strain out excess water.
  2. In a large cold frying pan, combine beef mince, cabbage, ginger, spring onion, Sichuan pepper, sesame oil, soy sauce, and salt.
  3. Mix well with hands or a spatula until the mixture becomes sticky and cohesive.
  4. Divide into 12 even portions directly in the pan and shape each into a small ball with wet fingers.
  5. Place a wonton wrapper over each ball and press down to encase the meat.
  6. Place pan over medium-high heat and cook 3-5 minutes until bases are golden and crispy.
  7. Add 1/4 cup water to the pan and cover immediately with a lid. Steam for about 5 minutes until beef is cooked through and wrappers are tender.
  8. Garnish with black vinegar, chilli crisp, and spring onion. Serve immediately.
