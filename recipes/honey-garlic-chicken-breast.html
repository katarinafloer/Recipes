Honey Garlic Chicken Breast

Dinner · 4 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

  • 500g / 1 lb chicken breast, boneless and skinless (2 pieces)
  • salt and pepper
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3.5 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup honey

Date Cooked

  • 2026-10-13
  • 2026-10-22

Notes

Dust chicken with flour before searing -- it creates a crust so the sauce clings better. Can substitute cornstarch for gluten free.

Works with any protein: pork, beef, lamb, shrimp, tofu. Adjust cook times accordingly.

Instructions

  1. Cut chicken breasts in half horizontally to make 4 thin steaks. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Coat chicken in flour and shake off excess.
  3. Melt most of the butter in a large skillet over high heat, reserving about 1 tsp.
  4. Cook chicken 2-3 minutes until golden, flip and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Reduce heat to medium-high. Add garlic and remaining butter, stir briefly.
  6. Add vinegar, soy sauce, and honey. Stir and bring to a simmer.
  7. Simmer 1 minute until slightly thickened.
  8. Turn chicken to coat in sauce. Add a splash of water if sauce gets too thick.
  9. Remove from heat and serve immediately, drizzling remaining sauce over.
