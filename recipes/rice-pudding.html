Rice Pudding

Breakfast · 5 minutes · 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup short-grain white rice
  • 3 cups milk
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • pinch of salt
  • chopped fruit, to serve (banana, strawberry, or whatever you have)

Date Cooked

  • 2026-09-11

Instructions

  1. Combine the rice, milk, and salt in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring often to prevent sticking.
  3. Cook for 25-30 minutes, stirring regularly, until the rice is tender and the mixture is thick and creamy.
  4. Stir in the maple syrup. Taste and adjust sweetness.
  5. Serve warm topped with chopped fruit.
