## Notes

Use apple cider the drink, not apple cider vinegar.

Slice chuck roast into 5-6 cubes before searing -- better crust than searing whole.

Roast the garlic heads at 400F for 30-40 min while the pot roast braises at 325F -- start the garlic early so it's ready when the potatoes are done.

## Instructions

### Pot Roast

1. Preheat oven to 325F. Season chuck roast pieces generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add 2 tbsp olive oil, sear half the beef 2-3 minutes per side until deeply browned. Repeat with remaining beef. Remove and set aside.
3. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil, then onion, garlic, and sage. Saute 4-5 minutes until fragrant and slightly browned.
4. Deglaze with 1/2 cup apple cider, stirring until steam slows. Add remaining cider, dijon, and Worcestershire sauce.
5. Return beef to pot so it's fully submerged. Add bay leaves and carrots on top.
6. Bring to a boil, cover, and place in oven for 3-4 hours until beef is very tender.
7. Remove beef and carrots. Reduce remaining liquid over medium-high heat for 15-20 minutes into a sauce.
8. Serve beef and carrots over mashed potatoes with sauce drizzled over.

### Roasted Garlic Mashed Potatoes

1. Trim tops off garlic heads to expose cloves. Place on foil, drizzle with olive oil, season with salt, wrap tightly. Roast at 400F for 30-40 minutes until tender.
2. Boil potatoes in generously salted water for 20-25 minutes until fork tender. Drain.
3. Return potatoes to pot over low heat and mash. Add roasted garlic and mash to combine.
4. Turn off heat. Add warmed cream and butter, mash until smooth. Add more cream to desired consistency. Season with salt and pepper.
