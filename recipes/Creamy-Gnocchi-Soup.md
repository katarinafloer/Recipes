## Notes

Don't skip the lemon at the end -- it brightens everything. White miso (shiro miso) is milder and less salty than red.

Common swaps: kale instead of spinach (cook a bit longer), coconut milk or cashew cream instead of soy cream, cannellini beans or chickpeas added for protein.

Store in fridge up to 3 days, freeze up to 2 months.

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high. Add garlic, celery, onion, and carrot. Cook 3-5 minutes until golden.
2. Add flour and cook 2 minutes, stirring to toast.
3. Add bouillon cubes, water, cream, nutritional yeast, Italian seasoning, and miso. Simmer partially covered for 10 minutes.
4. Add gnocchi, spinach, and frozen peas. Cook 3-4 minutes until gnocchi and peas are done.
5. Serve with fresh lemon juice, black pepper, and chili flakes.
