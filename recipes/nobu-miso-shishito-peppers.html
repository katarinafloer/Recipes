Nobu Miso Shishito Peppers

Snack/Appetizers · 5 minutes · 2

Source

Ingredients

  • shishito peppers
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • sesame seeds, for serving
  • lemon wedges, for serving

Date Cooked

  • 2026-11-21

Notes

Do not add the sauce to the hot pan -- it will burn. Add peppers to the bowl with the sauce instead.

Add chili oil or chili flakes to the sauce if you want heat.

Instructions

  1. Rinse and drain shishito peppers so they are slightly wet.
  2. Heat a skillet over high heat. Add wet peppers to the dry pan and shake occasionally until they begin to blister.
  3. Lower heat to medium, add oil, and continue shaking until blistered all over.
  4. Meanwhile, mix miso paste, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and sugar in a heat-safe bowl until smooth.
  5. Add hot peppers to the bowl with the sauce and toss to coat. The heat of the peppers will melt the sugar and make the sauce glossy.
  6. Serve immediately topped with sesame seeds and lemon wedges on the side.
