## Notes

Let cool at least 10 minutes before eating -- they crisp up as they cool. Serve with soy sauce and chili crisp oil for dipping.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450F. Line a large baking pan with parchment and drizzle generously with olive oil.
2. Mix tuna, yogurt, soy sauce, chili crisp oil, and scallions until tuna is finely mashed.
3. Add water to a shallow bowl. Dip one rice paper at a time to soften, then lay flat on a clean surface.
4. Layer with sushi rice, tuna mixture, cucumber, carrot, shishito peppers, and cilantro.
5. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly like a burrito. Place on the prepared pan. Repeat with remaining wrappers.
6. Drizzle assembled rolls with olive oil. Bake 15 minutes, flip, then bake another 10-15 minutes until browned and crispy.
7. Cool at least 10 minutes before serving.
