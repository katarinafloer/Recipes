## Notes

Apples release pectin when cooked, which is a natural prebiotic good for gut health.

Good toppings: Greek yogurt, pumpkin spice, caramelized walnuts.

## Instructions

1. Add chopped apple, maple syrup, water, and cinnamon to a pot. Cover and simmer for about 15 minutes.
2. Add cornstarch and cook another 5-10 minutes until thickened. Remove stewed apples and set aside.
3. In the same pot, add oats, cinnamon, chia seeds, maple syrup, and non-dairy milk. Cook 5-10 minutes until desired consistency.
4. Serve oatmeal topped with stewed apples and any toppings you like.
