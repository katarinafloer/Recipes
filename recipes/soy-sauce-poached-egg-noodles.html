Soy Sauce Poached Egg Noodles

Breakfast · 5 minutes · 1

Source

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • somyeon (thin wheat noodles)
  • 1 tbsp green onion, chopped
  • 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
  • 0.5 tbsp tuna fish sauce (or tsuyu)
  • 0.5 tbsp dark soy sauce (optional)
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • minced garlic, small amount
  • red pepper flakes
  • black pepper
  • toasted sesame seeds

Notes

The key trick: pour boiling water over the egg in its shell and let it sit 1 minute before cracking into the pot. This helps the egg white hold together for a clean poach.

Use 3 ladles of the noodle cooking water in the sauce -- it's starchy and ties everything together.

5 minutes covered = firm-but-soft yolk. 3 minutes = runnier yolk.

Instructions

  1. Pour boiling water over the egg (still in shell) so it's fully submerged. Let sit 1 minute.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil, then reduce heat until water stops moving (low heat).
  3. Gently crack the pre-soaked egg into the still water.
  4. Cover with a lid and cook 5 minutes for a firm-but-soft yolk (3 minutes for runnier). Remove and set aside.
  5. Boil the somyeon noodles in the same pot.
  6. In a bowl, combine green onion, soy sauce, tuna fish sauce, dark soy sauce, sesame oil, and minced garlic.
  7. Add 3 ladles of the noodle cooking water to the sauce.
  8. Drain noodles and add to the bowl along with the poached egg.
  9. Finish with red pepper flakes, black pepper, and toasted sesame seeds.
