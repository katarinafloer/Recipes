Swedish Meatballs

Lunch/Dinner · 30 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

  • 500g minced beef
  • 500g minced pork
  • 3/4 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • salt
  • butter
  • olive oil
  • fresh thyme and rosemary
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 5 Yukon potatoes
  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1-2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups lingonberries or cranberries
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • lemon juice, to taste
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 cups white wine vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp mustard seeds
  • dill

Notes

Linger/cranberry note: lingonberries are traditional and most IKEAs sell them. Cranberries are a substitute but not the same. Chill meatballs for 1 hour before cooking to help them hold shape.

Instructions

### Meatballs

  1. Combine panko, heavy cream, milk, egg, and Dijon in a bowl. Mix and let sit 5 minutes.
  2. Add ground beef, ground pork, allspice, salt, and pepper. Mix until well combined. Form into golf-ball-sized meatballs and chill 1 hour.
  3. Sear meatballs in a pan with olive oil. Butter baste with thyme and rosemary until fully cooked. Remove from pan.

### Gravy

  1. In the same pan, reduce beef stock with heavy cream until thickened. Whisk in Dijon mustard.
  2. Return meatballs to pan and warm in the gravy before serving.

### Mashed Potatoes

  1. Boil potatoes from cold water for about 35 minutes until tender. Peel while warm and pass through a sieve.
  2. Add to a pan over low heat and gradually fold in butter until creamy. Pour in milk, season with salt, and fold gently.

### Cranberry Sauce

  1. Cook lingonberries/cranberries in a pan over medium heat with sugar and lemon juice until jam-like. Do not squish the berries.

### Quick Pickles

  1. In a saucepan over low heat, dissolve sugar with white wine vinegar, water, and mustard seeds.
  2. Add sliced cucumbers and dill. Transfer to a container.
