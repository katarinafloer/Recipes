## Notes

Can also use other nut/seed butters (hazelnut, peanut) in place of tahini.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 180C/350F. Line a loaf pan with parchment.
2. Whisk eggs until well beaten. Add tahini, salt, and baking soda and mix until smooth.
3. Pour into the prepared loaf pan.
4. Bake about 30 minutes until set and golden on top.
5. Cool before slicing.
