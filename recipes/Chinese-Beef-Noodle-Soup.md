## Notes

Full recipe on the creator's Substack. Blanch the beef first and discard the first boil water to remove impurities before making the broth.

## Instructions

1. Blanch beef shanks and rib fingers in boiling water for a few minutes. Drain and rinse.
2. In a large pot, saute doubanjiang until fragrant. Add garlic, ginger, and scallions.
3. Add tomatoes, carrots, and onion. Cook until softened.
4. Add the beef back in along with soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, rock sugar, cooking wine, and all spices.
5. Cover with water and bring to a boil. Skim any foam.
6. Reduce heat and simmer 2-3 hours until beef is very tender.
7. Remove beef and slice or shred. Strain broth and season to taste.
8. Cook fresh noodles and blanch spinach separately.
9. Assemble bowls: noodles, beef, spinach, pickled mustard greens, soft-boiled egg, scallions, and cilantro. Ladle hot broth over top.
