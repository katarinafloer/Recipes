## Notes

No lingering fish smell since it bakes in parchment. Serve with couscous or rice to soak up all the juices.

10 min for medium-rare, 15 min for well done.

## Instructions

1. Season salmon with salt and set aside.
2. In a bowl, mix tomatoes, shallot, garlic, parsley, lemon juice, wine, olive oil, oregano, capers, caper brine, and pepper. Taste - it should be a little winey and bright.
3. Cut a large rectangle of parchment for each fillet. Place salmon in the center.
4. Spoon the tomato mixture and all its juices over the salmon. Add a small pat of butter on top.
5. Fold the parchment over, crimping and pinching to create a tight sealed packet. Twist the ends to lock in the juices.
6. Bake at 400F for 10-15 minutes depending on doneness.
7. Rest 5 minutes before opening - the steam will be hot. Serve with rice or couscous.
