Chilli Crisp Silken Tofu Shrimp Eggs

Lunch/Dinner · 5 minutes · 1

Notes

Optional rice: cook jasmine rice with lentils, beansprouts, dashi, soy sauce and lemongrass in a rice cooker with bok choy steamed on top.

Instructions

  1. Heat chilli crisp in a pan and add the silken tofu. Cut or poke holes in it and let it crisp up slightly.
  2. Add minced onion, garlic, and ginger. Cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Add shrimp, cover, and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Beat eggs with a splash of mirin and pour in. Cook for 3 minutes.
  5. Mix sauce ingredients (chilli crisp, Sichuan chilli oil, Kewpie mayo, water) and pour over.
  6. Finish with sliced scallions and serve with rice.
