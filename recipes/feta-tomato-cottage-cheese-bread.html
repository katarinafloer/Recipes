Feta & Tomato Cottage Cheese Bread

Breakfast · 5 minutes · 1

Source

Ingredients

  • 3 heaped tbsp (60g) self-raising flour (or plain flour + 1/2 tsp baking powder)
  • 2 tbsp (50g) cottage cheese
  • 1 tbsp feta, chopped
  • 1 tbsp tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried basil

Date Cooked

Not logged yet.

Notes

Dough should clump together and not be sticky - add more flour if needed.

Air fryer at 180C for 20 min, or oven at 180C for 35 min. If not cooked through, flip and cook 3-5 more minutes without parchment.

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, cottage cheese, feta, tomato, and basil in a bowl until it clumps together.
  2. Use your hands to squeeze into a boule shape. Score the top with a sharp knife.
  3. Air fry at 180C for 20 minutes, or bake at 180C for 35 minutes.
  4. If the middle is still soft, flip and cook another 3-5 minutes directly on the rack.
