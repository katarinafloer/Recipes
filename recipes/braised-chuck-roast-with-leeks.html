Braised Chuck Roast with Leeks

Dinner · 20 minutes · 6

Source

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chuck roast, cut into large 3-inch chunks
  • 1-2 shallots, quartered
  • 5-6 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 1/3 cup dry white wine (such as Chardonnay)
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 3 bay leaves
  • pinch of ground turmeric (optional)
  • water as needed
  • 3-4 medium leeks, cut into 1.5-inch pieces
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1.5 to 2 cups reserved braising liquid
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil (avocado oil)
  • olive oil
  • fresh parsley, for serving
  • salt and pepper

Notes

Cut leeks into 1.5-inch pieces (not larger - they braise better at that size).

The leeks almost melt into the braising liquid. Use the reserved liquid to braise them separately after the beef.

Could use beef stock instead of water for a richer braise.

Instructions

  1. Season chuck roast generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat neutral oil in a large heavy pot or Dutch oven over high heat. Sear the beef chunks on all sides until deeply browned. Work in batches to avoid crowding. Remove and set aside.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Add shallots and garlic and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Deglaze with white wine, scraping up the browned bits.
  5. Return the beef to the pot. Add peppercorns, bay leaves, and turmeric if using. Add enough water to come about halfway up the beef.
  6. Bring to a simmer, cover, and braise on low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours until the beef is very tender and falling apart.
  7. Remove beef and strain the braising liquid. Reserve 1.5 to 2 cups.
  8. In a wide pan, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the leeks, season with salt, pepper, and thyme. Pour in the reserved braising liquid.
  9. Simmer uncovered for 20-25 minutes until leeks are very soft and the liquid has reduced.
  10. Serve beef over or alongside the braised leeks. Drizzle with olive oil and garnish with parsley and fresh thyme. Serve with crusty bread.
