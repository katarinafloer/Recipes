## Notes

Peel the eggplant in a stripe pattern (every other 1/2 inch) so it stays tender but holds together.

Allow to stand 20-30 minutes before cutting so the pieces come out firm and clean.

Can be frozen before baking - thaw overnight in the fridge and bake when ready.

## Instructions

1. Wash the eggplant and trim the ends. Peel lengthwise every other 1/2 inch to create a stripe pattern. Slice into 1/2 inch circles and lay on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Toss with oil and bake at 400°F for 45 minutes until soft.
2. Meanwhile, peel the potatoes and boil for 30-40 minutes until fork tender. Mash with butter, milk, salt, and pepper until smooth and creamy.
3. For the meat sauce, sauté the diced onions in oil until soft, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and ground beef and cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes. Add all the seasoning and pasta sauce and cook for a few more minutes. Taste and adjust seasoning.
4. Layer in a baking dish: eggplant first, then meat sauce, then mashed potato. Add the potato in spoonfuls and gently flatten. Create grooves on top with a fork.
5. Bake uncovered at 400°F for 25 minutes, then broil for 5 minutes to crisp the top.
6. Allow to cool for 15-20 minutes before cutting and serving.
