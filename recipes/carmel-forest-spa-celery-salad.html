Carmel Forest Spa Celery Salad

Snack/Appetizers · 15 minutes · 4

Source

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch celery, chopped
  • 1 large honeycrisp apple, diced
  • 1 large cucumber, diced
  • 1 bunch fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • handful microgreens
  • 8-10 pitted medjool dates, chopped
  • 1/2 cup pecans, chopped
  • 1.5 cups parmesan, shaved
  • 1 bunch fresh dill, chopped
  • 2-3 large lemons, juiced
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup avocado oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Add celery, apple, cucumber, parsley, microgreens, dates, and pecans to a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix together parmesan, dill, lemon juice, mayonnaise, avocado oil, salt, pepper, garlic, and maple syrup until smooth.
  3. Pour dressing over salad and toss to combine.
