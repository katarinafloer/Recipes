## Notes

Don't crowd the pan when crisping the gnocchi or they'll steam instead of brown. Work in batches if needed.

Holds four days in the fridge and reheats well in a skillet or air fryer.

## Instructions

1. Heat butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the gnocchi in a single layer and cook undisturbed for about 8 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and crispy. Remove and set aside.
2. In the same pan, brown the diced ham for 2-3 minutes.
3. Add garlic and chili crisp and cook for 1 minute.
4. Toss the gnocchi back in along with lemon zest, lemon juice, parsley, and a generous amount of parmesan.
5. Season with black pepper and salt, toss to combine, and serve immediately.
