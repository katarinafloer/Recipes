## Notes

No-knead recipe — minimal effort for a loaf with a rich dark crust and soft chewy interior.

Can substitute rye flour with whole wheat flour or just use all white bread flour.

## Instructions

1. Stir together the flours, salt, and yeast in a large mixing bowl. Add the water and mix until no bits of dry flour remain. The dough will be very sticky.
2. Cover the bowl with plastic and let the dough rise at room temperature overnight until doubled in size, about 12 to 18 hours.
3. Generously dust a work surface with flour. Use a rubber spatula or fingertips to gently remove the dough from the bowl without tearing it. Lightly flour your hands and gently fold the dough into a ball.
4. Generously dust a tea towel with flour and place the dough onto it seam side down. Loosely fold the towel over the dough and place in a small mixing bowl. Let rise until almost doubled, about 1 to 2 hours.
5. Half an hour before the second rise finishes, place a rack in the lower third of the oven, set a Dutch oven on the rack, and preheat to 475°F.
6. Carefully remove the preheated Dutch oven and lid. Invert the dough into the pot seam side up. Cover and bake for 30 minutes.
7. Remove the lid and continue baking until dark golden brown, about 15 to 20 minutes.
8. Lift the bread out and let cool on a rack for at least 20 minutes before slicing.
